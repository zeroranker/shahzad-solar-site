Twelve checks · any installer · including us

Run this against us. Especially us.

Solar in Pakistan has a documented fraud problem. A Senate panel found over Rs 110 billion of fraud and money laundering across solar imports by 80 companies, and the FBR has filed cases against fake solar firms. Separately, a practitioner account written from Faisalabad documented a steady stream of rooftop thefts across the city.

Those are not arguments against solar. They are arguments for the twelve checks below. Tick them off on your phone, or print the page and take it to the site visit. Section three applies this list to us, including the two places we do not come out cleanly.

01 — The list

Twelve checks.

The "How" line under each one is the part most people skip. A check you cannot perform is not a check.

0 of 12 checked

02 — Money

The structure we ask for, so you have something to compare against.

Common in this market

  • Advance50–100%
  • Paid before anything is deliveredShould be nothing, or only materials already on your roof
  • Final paymentOn the day of installation

What we ask for

  • Advance30%
  • Paid againstAn itemised quote and an NTN invoice
  • Second40% on delivery of equipment
  • Final 30%After commissioning and handover
What the final third is actually for. It is released only when the system has been shown to you working. That is the single most effective protection in this list, because it is the only point at which you can still walk away without losing everything.

Two places we do not come out cleanly.
