Coming to see us

What to bring, and what to ask first.

Bring

Three or four consecutive FESCO bills — the more the better, because one month tells us very little. If you want solar for a pump, bring the pump's details instead: horsepower, depth, and how many hours a day it runs.

Ask first

"What is the regulation number you are sizing to?" It should be Regulation 3(2) of S.R.O. 251(I)/2026. If the answer is anything about 1.5×, the quotation was written before February and it is wrong.

Then ask

"What happens if the system underperforms, and where is that written?" If the answer is only a conversation, keep it in your pocket.

We are on Jhang Road, which is where most of Faisalabad's solar trade is, and where you will be able to compare us with three other installers in under an hour. That is deliberate. We would rather you did that than found us first.
