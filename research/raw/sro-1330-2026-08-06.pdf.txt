Islamabad, the 06th day of August, 2026
NOTIFICATION
S.R.O. I32& (I)/2026. In exercise of the powers conferred by section 47 of the Regulation of 
Generation, Transmission and Distribution of Electric Power Act, 1997 (XL of 1997) read with all other 
enabling provisions thereof, the National Electric Power Regulatory Authority hereby notifies following 
amendments in the National Electric Power Regulatory Authority (Prosumer) Regulations, 2026.-
1. In proviso to sub-regulation (11) of regulation 3, after the word “concurrence”, the words “or 
approval” shall be inserted.
2. In sub-regulation (1) of regulation 4, after first proviso, a second proviso shall be inserted as follows:
“Provided that a prosumer having distributed generation facility of 25 kW or below capacity 
shall not be required to seek concurrence from the Authority and the concerned licensee shall 
accord its approval.”
3. In sub-regulation (4) of regulation 4,-
j. after the word “concurrence” occurring for the first time, the words “or approval” shall be inserted; 
and
ii. after the word “concurrence” occurring for the second time, the words “or approval as the case 
may be” shall be inserted.
4. In clause 4 of the “Distributed Generation Interconnection Agreement” provided in the Schedule-I, 
after the words “for the facility”, the words “if applicable” shall be inserted.
