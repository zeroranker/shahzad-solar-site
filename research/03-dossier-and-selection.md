**Why ElectroTech lost despite the richest material.** It has the best-documented substance of any
candidate — 20 years, PEC/AEDB/FBR/chamber affiliations, named CEO, named industrial clients including
Coca-Cola and Interloop — and it already runs a working site (`etsolar.pk`). The mismatch between
real-world value and digital experience is real but modest: a dated WordPress site is an *improvement*
opportunity, not an *absence* opportunity. Building it a third property would compete with its own.

**Why Shahzad Solar won.** The mismatch is total and unusually well-evidenced:
- A business with a documented **1 MW**, **250 kW**, **150 kW** and **70 kW** project record;
- A **332,623-follower** parent pump brand and a **103,230-follower** solar page at the same premises;
- **Zero working website of any kind.** Its own Facebook links to `shahzadsolar.com`, which is
  **NXDOMAIN** on Google DNS, Cloudflare DNS and the local resolver. Its own advertised email
  `info@shahzadsolar.com` is therefore **undeliverable** — a verified, hard technical failure, not an
  inference;
- A **Google Business Profile** that lists no website;
- All proof trapped inside Facebook, Instagram and YouTube where it cannot be evaluated, compared,
  shared, or found in a search result.

That is the second-order insight the mission asks for: *the evidence exists and is real — it is
simply in the wrong container.* Nobody can be found, nobody can be checked, nobody can be compared.

## PART B — THE "TRY TO DISPROVE IT" TEST

| Attack | Result |
|---|---|
| "They already have a website." | **No.** The only claimed domain is NXDOMAIN, confirmed by three independent resolvers. |
| "The 103K followers are bought, so the business is weak." | **Partly valid — and it changed the design.** Facebook 103,230 vs Instagram 474 is a ~217:1 ratio, atypical. YouTube is 142 subs with 15–322 views per video. **Consequence: the follower count is not used anywhere on the site as a trust signal.** The build rests on verifiable project records, a physical address, and a live parent company instead. |
| "The projects are self-reported and might be fiction." | **They remain unverified — and are labelled as such.** The site attributes every project to Shahzad Solar's own published video and links to the source, so the buyer can check. No project is presented as independently certified. |
| "Different 'Shahzad Solar' companies exist." | **No evidence of a duplicate.** No conflicting entity, address or phone found. |
| "It is a one-man technician outfit." | **Disproved.** Same premises as Shahzad Pumps (160-A / Sheikh Colony, Jhang Road, near Rescue 1122), a Faisalabad pump manufacturer "since 2015" with a live site, 332k followers and a LinkedIn company page. |
| "The '45 years' claim." | **Rejected.** The group's own website says *since 2015*. The 45-year line appears in one Urdu video and is not corroborated. **It is not used on the site.** |
| "A website is unnecessary — Facebook already sells for them." | **Disproved by their own data.** A buyer who arrives from a Facebook reel cannot verify capacity, cannot see a specification, cannot get net-billing guidance, and cannot email (the address is dead). Facebook converts attention; it cannot answer the questions that decide a Rs 1.3M purchase. |
| "Someone better was overlooked." | Checked. ElectroTech is stronger on substance but weaker on gap. The selection stands. |

**Survived.** Proceeding.

### Strengths (observable)
1. A real, verifiable industrial track record at the exact capacity tier the 2026 rules reshaped.
2. Physical premises, verifiable on a map, shared with an established pump manufacturer since 2015.
3. Reaches its audience — 103k on Facebook — but has no destination to send them to.
4. Genuine agricultural capability through the pump business, in a district full of tubewells.
5. Active in Urdu; the audience is mobile-first and Facebook-native.

### Weaknesses (observable)
1. No website; claimed domain unregistered; published email undeliverable.
2. No addressable content: nothing to rank, nothing to share, nothing to compare.
3. No explanation of the Feb 2026 net-billing change — their highest-value customers face it now.
4. No written specification or quote standard, in a market whose #1 documented failure is exactly that.
5. English-language web presence is effectively zero, so they are invisible to anyone who searches.

### Risks — the website must NOT claim
- 45 years in solar (unsupported; group says since 2015).
- That the 1 MW / 250 kW / 150 kW projects are independently certified (they are company-published).
- Guaranteed net-billing approval, connection, or timeline (NEPRA/DISCO-controlled; documented 2–4+ months).
- "Zero bill" (fixed charges still apply).
- "Tier-1" as a certification (the market's own sources call this term unstandardised).
- Any follower count, client count, or years-in-business number not evidenced above.
- Any unverified saving percentage or payback figure.

### Information gaps — must be confirmed by the owner before launch
1. Is "Shahzad Solar (Private) Limited" the correct legal entity, and what is the NTN?
2. Which phone number is current and which is the WhatsApp business line?
3. Is `0321-6660744` still live, or superseded?
4. Is `shahzadsolar.com` intended to be registered — may the site be deployed there?
5. Brand lineups actually supplied (panel, inverter, battery brands) — **not yet verified, so not claimed.**
6. Project capacities, dates and client permissions to publish/embed.
7. Team size, technician count, and who signs off designs.
8. Warranty terms in writing, per component.
9. Whether agricultural solar-tubewell work is offered directly or via Shahzad Pumps.
10. Service area beyond Faisalabad.
